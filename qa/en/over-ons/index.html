About us

Built by
entrepreneurs,
for entrepreneurs.

It started with a frustration. We saw how local businesses — the butcher on the corner, the plumber who's always available, the hairdresser who has cut the same clients' hair for years — were completely invisible online.

Large agencies charged €5,000 for a website that took months to deliver. So we built DRP BuildLab: a studio that believes every local business deserves a custom website — affordable, honest, with a price that matches the work it takes.

01
Honesty above all
What we promise, we deliver. No hidden costs, no vague quotes.
02
Maintenance when you want it
Monthly maintenance is optional: €29 per month or €250 per year. Add it and your site stays secure, up to date and findable — without you having to think about it.
03
Think local, build digital
We build for local businesses — the ones whose customers are searching nearby. We work remotely from Belgium, so where you are does not decide whether we can help.
